About aSuggestion

Introducing aSuggestion for Wellness & Care

aSuggestion was built with autism and I/DD agencies to give care teams better tools, and the people they support a safe place to connect and grow.

Welcome to aSuggestion. Better Tools for Care Teams, a Safe Space for Everyone They Support.

We get it.

If you lead or work in an agency supporting people with Autism (ASD) or an Intellectual or Developmental Disability (I/DD), you already know the pressure: not enough hands, more documentation every year, audits to pass, and outcomes to show your funders. Meanwhile the people you support can go long stretches between visits with little structure or connection.

For the individuals themselves, and for their families, navigating the world can feel complicated and isolating. They are looking for a place to connect, to share, and to learn, without fear of judgment, and to find people who truly understand. Co-occurring behavioral health needs are part of that picture too.

That's why we built aSuggestion for Wellness & Care.

We're not just another social media app. We are a Digital Health Overlay: a private, invitation-only community built from the ground up to sit on top of the systems your agency already uses, with one mission: a safe, supportive, and truly helpful ecosystem for the people you support, their families, and your entire care team.

  • • Safety is Everything: This is our number one promise. Membership is by invitation only, set up at your agency's direction. Every post, comment, and message is reviewed by AI moderation, and your own care team is alerted the moment something needs a human look. Member posts and comments are never AI-generated, and we test the AI moderation every single week to make sure it never quietly stops working.
  • • Privacy Built In, Not Bolted On: For every piece of field data a care team captures, including visit records, activity logs, and exports, we designed the system from the ground up to avoid storing addresses or protected health information at all. Staff and organization exports use a permanent, de-identified ID instead of a name.
  • • A Place for Everyone: The people you support can join themed Social Rooms to talk about hobbies, life skills, or just hang out. Families and caregivers are welcome too, with their own private areas to connect, share experiences, and learn from each other.
  • • Built With Agencies, For Agencies: Features come straight from partner feedback, so the tools match how shifts, visits, and audits actually work. Billing follows real use: you pay only for the people who are active each month.
  • • Learn and Grow, Actively: Our tools don't just play videos at you. Interactive AI cooking guides, storytelling, coloring, and daily living checklists turn learning into something you actually do, with matching tools for caregivers to document and support that growth.
  • • Real Connection: Our goal is to bridge the gaps between visits and when in-person care is hard to find, so people feel supported anytime, anywhere.

aSuggestion. Life Improved.

About aSuggestion

Introducing aSuggestion for Wellness & Care

We are building a safer digital space for meaningful conversations around mental and behavioral health, connecting individuals, families, caregivers, professionals, and partner agencies through empathy and trust.

Welcome to aSuggestion. A Safe Space to Connect, Learn, and Grow.

We get it.

Navigating the world with Autism (ASD), an Intellectual or Developmental Disability (I/DD), or other mental health challenges can feel... complicated. Whether you're an individual looking for connection, a family member seeking answers, or a caregiver trying to find the best resources, it's easy to feel isolated.

You're looking for a place to connect, to share, and to learn — without fear of judgment. A place where you can be yourself and find people who truly understand.

That's why we built aSuggestion for Wellness & Care.

We're not just another social media app. We are a private digital community built from the ground up with one mission: to provide a safe, supportive, and truly helpful ecosystem for you, your family, and your entire care team.

  • • Safety is Everything: This is our number one promise. Our community is a "predator-free" zone. We use a powerful combination of trained human moderators and smart AI to ensure every conversation is respectful, constructive, and safe.
  • • A Place for Everyone: Individuals can join themed "social rooms" to talk about hobbies, life skills, or just hang out. Families and caregivers get their own private areas to connect, share experiences, and learn from each other.
  • • Learn and Grow: We have libraries packed with videos, guides, and activities to help with daily living skills, emotional regulation, and so much more — with matching content for caregivers too.
  • • Real Connection: Our goal is to bridge the gaps when in-person care is hard to find, helping you feel supported anytime, anywhere.

Welcome to aSuggestion for Wellness & Care

Most agencies don't need another system to replace. They need a layer that makes the systems they already have more effective.

aSuggestion is a Digital Health Overlay: it sits on top of your existing operations, with nothing to rip out and nothing to migrate. It combines a private, moderated community for the people you support with the documentation, goal-tracking and reporting tools your staff need, built for Medicaid waiver and HCBS programs.

Every tool connects back to the two things that matter most: each person's goals and your billable events. Staff spend less time on paperwork and more time with the people they support, and leaders get real visibility into engagement and outcomes.

For the people you support, it's a safe place to connect, explore interests and build everyday skills such as communication, job readiness, emotional regulation and daily living. Families and caregivers are welcome too, with the same welcoming environment and tools to stay involved.

Behind the scenes, AI moderation reviews every post, comment and message, and alerts your care team the moment something needs a human look.

One overlay. Three layers of value: Engage, Equip, Document.

Engage · Participant layer

Moderated Social Rooms, games and activities, and live Check-In Chats that give people a reason to log in every day.

Equip · Care team layer

A treasure chest of tools to plan, run and track programs, all tied to Person Centered Goals.

Document · Compliance layer

Every session, outing and interaction becomes timestamped, exportable evidence linked to billable events.

For Agencies & Care Teams

Everything your team needs to document, bill, and show outcomes

Ready-to-use tools that help your staff facilitate and track programs, built alongside your existing systems, not in place of them. Every one ties back to Person Centered Goals and your agency's billable events.

Guided Notes

Structured, audit-ready service documentation built for real shift workflows, so notes capture what happened and why it matters to the plan.

De-Identified Exports

Pull complete activity histories into Excel or Google Sheets, with staff and organization exports built around permanent de-identified IDs instead of names.

Built-In Day Program Content

A ready-made library of games, guided crafts, cooking, and curated videos. Start a session on any activity and the time is captured toward billable Day Program hours, with no separate timesheet.

Person Centered Goals

Choose goals from a curated 100-item library and let evidence surface automatically from real activity, so progress is visible, not just assumed.

Visit Trac

Confirms arrival and departure with the time and a location type, never an address. An audit-ready internal record that works alongside your state's EVV system.

Shared Tasks and Reminders

Assign and track tasks and reminders for a specific person, so nothing about their care depends on one staff member remembering it.

And much more, such as . . .

Community Instruction

Plan and document community outings, from grocery runs to library visits. Mileage is estimated automatically, and group photos are shared with consent built in.

Video Library and Live Events

A curated library of videos and live events, shared by your organization and the wider community.

Multilanguage & Accessibility

Posts and comments translate into 75+ languages, the interface is available in English and Spanish, and dark mode and keyboard-accessible controls are built in.

Check-In Chats

A live, secure video check-in between a team member and the person they support, started in a click. Sessions are transcribed for your care team.

Wellbeing Dashboard

Trends in mood, participation and activity at a glance, so a quiet drop-off never goes unnoticed.

Engagement Levels

See who is participating, who is drifting and where to follow up.

Skill-Building Games

Emotion Matching, Routine Sequencing, Weather Wardrobe, Money Management and more, each building a real-life skill.

From touchpoint to record

1. It happens. Any touchpoint across the system in any setting.

2. It's verified. Visit Trac confirms arrival and departure with the time and a location type.

3. It's documented. Notes, summaries and goal evidence attach to the person.

4. It's ready. Export to Excel, Google Sheets or PDF, ready for your EHR.

Pay only for engaged users

You are billed monthly for the people who actively use the platform, not for seats that sit idle. Start with a private pilot sandbox: explore everything with your own team, then go live without re-entering anything.

Welcome to aSuggestion for Wellness & Care

A Digital Health Overlay where connection, growth, and support come together.

At aSuggestion, we believe that everyone deserves access to meaningful connection, personal development, and emotional support. If you're an individual with autism, an intellectual or developmental disability, a mental or behavioral health challenge, or another form of neurodiversity, this is your space to connect, share, explore interests, and grow, safely and without judgment. Families get the same welcoming environment, plus tools to stay informed and involved in what matters most.

This is more than just another social media platform. aSuggestion is a Digital Health Overlay that blends real social engagement with structured learning and practical resources, built alongside the systems agencies already use, not in place of them. For caregivers and care teams, that means practical, audit-ready tools for the realities of daily care. For agency leadership, it means real visibility into engagement and outcomes across everyone you support.

For those seeking personal growth, the platform offers interactive activities and guides focused on building essential life skills such as communication, job readiness, emotional regulation, and daily living. Families, caregivers, and agencies can access educational materials, community discussions, structured documentation tools, and de-identified reporting built for the way care actually happens.

Behind the scenes, our AI and moderation tools work constantly to maintain a safe environment, providing gentle guidance and structure that helps everyone stay supported and engaged.

aSuggestion is more than a platform. It is a community that helps people learn, connect, and grow together.

For Organizations & Care Teams

The audit-ready toolset your care team already needs

aSuggestion isn't only a community for the people you support. It's also a standalone set of field tools for the staff who support them, built alongside your existing systems, not in place of them.

Guided Notes

Structured, audit-ready service documentation built for real shift workflows, not a blank text box.

Visit Trac

Audit-ready visit documentation capturing the data elements required under the 21st Century Cures Act, with no addresses or PHI stored.

Wellbeing Dashboard

A real-time view into engagement and wellbeing trends across everyone your team supports.

Person Centered Goals

A curated 100-item goal library with an attainment report, so progress toward real goals is visible, not just assumed.

De-Identified Exports

CSV exports built around permanent, de-identified IDs instead of real names, from day one.

Shared To-Dos

Assign and track tasks for a specific person, so nothing about their care depends on one staff member remembering it.

Excursion Tracking

Log community outings, calculate mileage automatically, and publish group photos with participant-level consent built in.

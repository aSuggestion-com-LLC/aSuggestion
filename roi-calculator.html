1. Your agency

aSuggestion is billed only for people who actively use the platform in a given month. A month with no use costs nothing.

2. Your waitlist New revenue

People waiting for waiver funding average 38 months on a list nationally. Engage them on aSuggestion now, build their goals and a record of their needs, and be the agency they already know when funding opens. Many are also eligible today for services that don't need a waiver slot, such as state plan or 1915(i) supports, Vocational Rehabilitation transition services, or family-paid programs.

3. People you turn away for lack of staff New revenue

Uses the DSP Pipeline hires you enter in section 4.

4. DSP Pipeline hires Savings

Pipeline interns arrive vetted, trained and already experienced with the people you support, so we assume they stay longer than a typical new hire. Nationally, 64% of DSPs who left in 2024 did so within their first year.

5. Documentation and admin time Savings

Time staff spend gathering observations, matching them to each person's goals and preparing documentation for billing. aSuggestion collects activity and goal evidence as it happens, ready for staff to use in your own EHR.

6. Services delivered but not captured Recovered revenue

7. Families you refer to aSuggestion New revenue

Individuals and families can join aSuggestion on their own for $30 per month. When a family names your agency as the one that referred them, $10 of that $30 each month goes to your agency, and your team works with them in the platform. Card processing fees come out of the $10.

This is an estimate based on the numbers you entered, not a guarantee of results. Waiver enrollment depends on your state's funding decisions, and people always choose their own provider. aSuggestion does not bill Medicaid or replace your EHR; it records activity and goal evidence your staff can use in your own documentation. The DSP Pipeline training fee is charged by our partner Potentia, which also helps agencies look for grant funding that may offset it.
